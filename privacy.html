Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data GMSim ("we", "the Site", available at gmsim.ca) collects when you use it, how we use that data, and what rights you have over it. We are an independent tool and are not affiliated with GoMining or its operators.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the minimum information needed to operate the Site:

  • Account information — when you sign in with Google or via an email magic link, we receive your email address, display name, and profile picture (Google sign-in only). This is provided by Google or by you.
  • Usage data — which sections of the Site you open, the duration of your session, how many page views you generate, and the timestamps of your sign-ins. This helps us understand which features people use most.
  • Calculator inputs you save — if you save a scenario or portfolio entry, that data is stored against your account so you can retrieve it later.
  • Device and connection metadata — IP address, browser user agent, and referrer (the page you came from). Standard server-side log information.

We do not collect your wallet keys, private keys, seed phrases, or any on-chain credentials. The Site never asks for them.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and keep you signed in.
  • To save your calculator scenarios and personal preferences.
  • To understand which features are popular and improve the Site.
  • To detect and prevent abuse (spam sign-ups, malicious activity).
  • To display advertising via Google AdSense.
  • To send you the strategy alert, and only that, if you switch it on yourself (see Communications below).

3. Service Providers

We use a small number of third-party services to run the Site. By using the Site, you also agree to their respective policies:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication + Firestore database) — stores your account record and usage statistics. Firebase Privacy.
  • Vercel — hosts the Site files and serves them to your browser. Vercel Privacy.
  • Google AdSense — serves contextual advertising. Google Ads Policy.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

The Site uses browser local storage and cookies for the following purposes:

  • Authentication — Firebase stores a session token so you stay signed in across visits.
  • Preferences — your language, theme, currency, and any cached calculator values.
  • Advertising — Google AdSense and its partners may set cookies to serve personalized or non-personalized ads.

You can clear cookies and local storage at any time via your browser settings. Doing so will sign you out and erase locally cached preferences.

5. Browser Extension (GMSim — Miner Sync)

We publish an optional Chrome extension, GMSim — Miner Sync, that copies your own GoMining miner data into the Site so you do not have to type it in by hand. Installing it is entirely optional; the Site works without it.

  • What it reads. While you are signed in on app.gomining.com, the extension observes the data your browser already receives there — over the page's own network calls and its live sockets alike. It keeps only what the simulator needs, from a fixed list of mining endpoints: your miners and their hashrate, power and efficiency, your reward history, your wallet balances and addresses, your account discounts, your veGMT lock, and the GMT and BTC prices GoMining quotes. On the Miner Wars pages it also keeps the live scoreboard shown to you, which includes other players' public in-game figures. It reads text already rendered on the page to recover your total farm power, because that figure is not always present in a network response. Responses from any other endpoint — sign-in and account, KYC, notifications, marketing, loans — are discarded without being stored.
  • Where that data goes. Nowhere except your own browser. It is written to the extension's local storage (chrome.storage.local) and copied into the local storage of gmsim.ca when you have the Site open. The extension contains no server, no analytics, and makes no network request of its own — it never transmits your data to us or to any third party.
  • What it never touches. It never stores your name, e-mail address, phone number, IP address, KYC status, session tokens, passwords, private keys or seed phrases, and it cannot move funds or act on your GoMining account. It is read-only.
  • Where it runs. Only on app.gomining.com (to read) and on gmsim.ca (to deliver). It is inactive on every other site you visit.
  • Retention and removal. Captured data auto-expires after 24 hours, and reward history is capped at 30 days. The extension's panel has a Purge button that erases everything on the spot. Uninstalling the extension deletes its stored data immediately; clearing site data for gmsim.ca removes the copy held there.

GMSim is an independent tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by GoMining.

6. Communications

We do not send marketing email. Creating an account does not put you on a mailing list, and we will not use the address you signed in with to promote anything to you.

There is exactly one email we may send, and only if you switch it on yourself:

  • Strategy alert — a note when the optimal strategy for your own miner changes, or when Bitcoin moves enough to change your returns meaningfully. It is off by default. You turn it on from the ⋯ menu on the dashboard, under Alerts, and you can turn it off in the same place at any time. Every message we send also carries a one-click unsubscribe link.

Separately from that, we may send service messages that are not promotional: a sign-in link when you request one, a reply when you write to us, or notice of a change that materially affects your account or this policy.

If you ever receive email from us that you did not ask for, tell us at the address in the Contact section and we will treat it as a fault on our side.

7. Advertising

The Site displays advertising served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site or other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

8. Data Retention

Account records and usage data are retained while your account is active. If you would like your account and all associated data deleted, email us at the address below — we will remove your record from Firebase within 30 days.

9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. EU/UK under GDPR, California under CCPA, Canada under PIPEDA), you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete the personal information we hold about you. To exercise any of these rights, email us at the address below.

10. Children

The Site is not directed to children under 13 (or under the age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

12. International Transfers

Our infrastructure is provided by Google and Vercel, which are headquartered in the United States and operate data centers globally. By using the Site, you consent to your information being processed outside your country of residence.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes. Material changes will be posted as a notice in-app.
